# Problem Statement

## Core Problem

Users lack a simple and fast method to analyze and select stocks based on their individual investment priorities. Without a streamlined tool, users struggle to identify top-performing stocks aligned with their specific financial criteria.

## Who Is Affected

- Individual retail investors
- Stock market participants seeking to make informed decisions
- Users seeking personalized stock analysis based on custom metrics

## Why the Problem Matters

- **Complexity:** Users find existing stock analysis tools overwhelming and difficult to navigate
- **Time:** Manual stock analysis is time-consuming and inefficient
- **Clarity:** Without clear prioritization options (ROI, growth, dividends), users cannot effectively compare stocks
- **Decision Quality:** Poor access to organized, top-performing stock data leads to suboptimal investment decisions
